CNGuard — Privacy Policy
Last updated: 25 September 2026

CNGuard is an app for CNG station staff that looks up a vehicle's hydrostatic test certificate on the Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organisation (PESO) public portal. This policy describes what the app collects, why, and where it goes.

What the app collects
Your name and mobile number. Entered once, when you first open the app. PESO's portal requires both to run a lookup: it sends a one-time verification code by SMS to the number you provide.

The vehicle numbers you look up, and the certificate data PESO returns for them.

The app does not collect or access: your location, your contacts, your photos, your SMS messages, your device advertising identifier, or any account credentials. It does not create an account and there is nothing to sign in to.

Where your data is stored
Your name, mobile number and lookup history are stored only on your own phone, in the app's private storage. They are not uploaded to any account, backed up to a server, or shared with anyone. Uninstalling the app deletes them. You can clear the history at any time from within the app, and change your name or mobile number in Settings.

What is sent over the network
To perform a lookup, the app sends to our server:

the vehicle number you typed,
your name and mobile number, because PESO's form requires them,
the one-time code PESO texted you.
Our server passes these to PESO's public portal, reads the resulting certificate page, and returns the certificate to your phone. It exists so that a change to PESO's website can be fixed on the server rather than requiring every phone to install a new version of the app.

Our server does not keep your data. A lookup holds a short-lived session (needed because PESO's portal is stateful) which is discarded automatically within ten minutes. Verification codes and mobile numbers are not written to logs. Nothing is stored after a lookup completes.

Third parties
Certificate data comes from PESO (online.peso.gov.in). Your name, mobile number and the vehicle number are submitted to PESO's portal as part of the lookup, exactly as they would be if you filled in that form yourself in a browser. PESO's own handling of that information is governed by PESO, not by us.

Our server runs on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare processes the request in transit and may log standard network metadata such as IP addresses, as any web host does.

There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no advertising network, and no tracking of any kind in this app.

Children
CNGuard is a workplace tool for CNG station staff. It is not directed at children and collects nothing from them.

Permissions
The app declares one Android permission: internet access. It does not request SMS, contacts, location, camera, or storage permissions. You read the verification code from your own notifications; the app never reads your messages.

Your choices
Change your name or mobile number: Settings, inside the app.
Delete your lookup history: Settings, inside the app.
Delete everything: uninstall the app. All local data goes with it.
Because nothing is stored on our side after a lookup, there is no server-side record for us to delete on request.

Not affiliated with PESO or IGL
CNGuard is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by PESO, Indraprastha Gas Limited (IGL), or any government body. Certificate data belongs to PESO and is displayed as published.
